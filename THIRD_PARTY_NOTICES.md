## Data stored in this repository

### Lunar feature names and positions (IAU / USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature)

- **Used for:** the names, centres and diameters of the 86 lunar features `astro-id moon` labels
  (`astro_id/moon_catalog.py`, generated by `tools/make_moon_catalog.py`).
- **By:** IAU Working Group for Planetary System Nomenclature (WGPSN), maintained by the USGS Astrogeology Science
  Center. https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/ , file `MOON_nomenclature_center_pts` from
  https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/GIS_Downloads
- **License:** public domain. The file's own metadata states "Use constraints: Public domain."; USGS-produced data
  are U.S. public domain. Credit: Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature, IAU WGPSN / USGS.

### Apollo landing-site coordinates (NASA NSSDCA)

- **Used for:** the six Apollo landing sites in `astro_id/moon_catalog.py`.
- **By:** NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (Lunar Module positions from LRO images, Wagner et al. 2017,
  *Icarus* 283, 92-103).
  https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_sites.html
- **License:** U.S. public domain (NASA).

### Full-Moon test image (NASA Scientific Visualization Studio)

- **Used for:** `examples/moon/05-nasa-svs-full-moon.jpg`, a 730 x 730 px frame of *Moon Phase and Libration, 2026*

  celestial north up).
- **By:** NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio (visualizer Ernie Wright). https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587

## Online services (only when used)

### astrometry.net (optional fallback)

- **Used for:** solving a frame ASTAP could not, only when `ASTROMETRY_API_KEY` is set and `--no-fallback` is not
  given. A downscaled grayscale copy is uploaded as a private, non-commercial submission.
- **By:** the Astrometry.net project (Dustin Lang, David W. Hogg et al.); nova.astrometry.net is hosted by the
  Canadian Advanced Network for Astronomical Research (CANFAR), operated by the Canadian Astronomy Data Centre.
  https://nova.astrometry.net/
- **Terms:** the service's own terms apply. Reference: Lang, D., Hogg, D. W., Mierle, K., Blanton, M., Roweis, S.
  (2010), *Astrometry.net: Blind astrometric calibration of arbitrary astronomical images*, AJ 139, 1782.

### USGS Elevation Point Query Service

- **Used for:** the elevation of an observing site in the United States (`astro-id site` and the panel's location step).
- **By:** U.S. Geological Survey, The National Map. https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/
- **License:** U.S. public domain. Credit: U.S. Geological Survey.
  https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits

### Google Maps and Google Earth

The site picker and the panel open Google Maps or Google Earth in your browser so you can find a location. astro-id
fetches no data from Google. Google's terms apply to your use of those sites.
